Fixed Korean → English mappings for the ITPE Knowledge Base. Extracted by frequency from
`content/` (185 files, ~1.03 MB).

**These are binding.** If a term appears here, use the listed English form — not a synonym,
not a paraphrase. Consistency across 185 files matters more than local elegance.

Where a mapping is marked *(keep acronym)*, the English acronym is already used in the Korean
source; carry it through unchanged.
