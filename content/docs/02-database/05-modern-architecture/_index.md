In the age of big data, cloud, and AI/LLM, database technology has evolved beyond the limitations of the relational model to take diverse forms. NoSQL, distributed processing frameworks, and cloud-native databases emerged to handle unstructured high-volume data generated by social media, IoT, and real-time streaming, and recently vector databases linked with LLMs are gaining attention.

This section covers the BASE characteristics of NoSQL that replace ACID in traditional RDBMS, four types of data models, the evolution of big-data storage from data warehouse to lakehouse, and cloud-native databases and specialized databases (time-series and vector).
