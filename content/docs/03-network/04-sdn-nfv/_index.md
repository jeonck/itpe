SDN, NFV, VxLAN, and SD-WAN are next-generation network paradigms that abstract and virtualize hardware-centric traditional networks through software to realize agility and scalability in the cloud era. Through separation of the control plane and data plane, network function virtualization, overlay tunneling, and intelligent WAN control, they enable vendors to break free from vendor lock-in and enable operational automation.
