This section covers the core technology systems for ensuring network quality through QoS and load balancing, and constructing next-generation infrastructure through CDN, satellite communication, and quantum key distribution. Students learn advanced topics spanning all layers of modern network architecture, from bandwidth control to quantum mechanics-based secure communication.
