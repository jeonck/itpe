Security governance and compliance are management frameworks for systematically managing an organization's information protection activities and meeting legal and regulatory requirements, covering risk identification, analysis, and response through certification acquisition and personal data protection compliance.

| Topic | Core Content | Key Keywords |
|---|---|---|
| **[Security Risk Management Process](risk-management)** | Four-step risk identification, analysis, assessment, response and ISO/IEC 27001 PDCA management system | ALE, risk matrix, BCP/DR, RTO/RPO |
| **[ISMS-P and CSAP Certification](isms-p-csap)** | Domestic integrated information protection certification ISMS-P 102 control items and public cloud CSAP grading system | ISMS-P, CSAP high/medium/low, mandatory certification |
| **[Personal Data Protection Law and PIA](privacy-law-pia)** | Personal data, pseudonymized information, anonymized information classification, My Data, privacy impact assessment (PIA), de-identification techniques | K-anonymity, differential privacy, PIA, My Data |
