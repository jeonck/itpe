Cloud computing has evolved from IaaS, PaaS, SaaS to cloud native architecture with containers, microservices, and serverless, establishing itself as the IT infrastructure standard. PE exams emphasize service and deployment model comparison, container orchestration, DevOps and CI/CD pipelines, and cost optimization, requiring systematic understanding of responsibility boundaries and design principles.
