Computational complexity theory mathematically classifies the resource (time and space) limits required to solve problems and is a core field of theoretical computer science exploring the fundamental difficulty of algorithms.

Understanding the relationships between P, NP, NP-Hard, and NP-Complete complexity classes enables distinguishing between problems that can be practically solved and those that cannot, and systematically establishing optimization, cryptography, and artificial intelligence design strategies.
